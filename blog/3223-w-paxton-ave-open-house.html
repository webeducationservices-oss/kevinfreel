Now Staged South Tampa Home With Closing Cost Incentive

One of the biggest updates to this listing is simple but important: this home is now staged and must be seen in person.

Staging helps buyers understand the layout, scale, and flow of a home. Empty rooms can feel difficult to judge. Staged rooms make it easier to see where furniture fits, how spaces connect, and how the home can work for daily life.

At 3223 W Paxton Ave, the staging brings out the home’s strongest features. The open living areas feel warm and usable. The bedrooms feel inviting. The kitchen, great room, and outdoor living space come together in a way that makes the home easier to experience.

Buyer Incentive: The seller will contribute up to $20,000 toward closing costs if closing in June 2026.

That can be a meaningful opportunity for qualified buyers. Closing costs can include lender fees, title fees, prepaid taxes, insurance, and other expenses tied to the purchase. A seller contribution may help reduce the amount a buyer needs to bring to closing, depending on loan type, terms, and lender approval.

For buyers already looking in South Tampa, this incentive adds another reason to take a closer look now. A staged, move-in-ready custom home with a June closing cost contribution is not something every listing offers.

First Floor Features at 3223 W Paxton Ave

The first floor of this custom South Tampa home was designed with comfort, flexibility, and everyday function in mind.

As you enter, the 10-foot ceilings create an immediate sense of space. The home feels bright, open, and airy. High ceilings can make a major difference in how a home lives, especially when paired with large windows and an open layout.

One of the most useful first-floor features is the bedroom or office with a closet and full bathroom. This room gives buyers options. It can serve as a guest suite, private office, playroom, hobby room, or space for multigenerational living.

For buyers who work from home, this room is especially valuable. It provides separation from the main living areas while still being easy to access. For guests, the nearby full bath makes overnight stays more comfortable.

The chef’s kitchen is one of the main highlights of the home. It has the kind of finishes today’s buyers look for in a luxury South Tampa property.

The kitchen is both beautiful and practical. The large island creates space for meal prep, casual dining, entertaining, and everyday conversation. The built-in double ovens are ideal for buyers who cook often or host family and friends. The pot filler adds convenience, while the extended pantry provides much-needed storage.

The spacious great room continues the open feel. Large windows bring in natural light, and the floating credenza gives the room a clean, finished look. This is the kind of space that works just as well for quiet evenings as it does for hosting guests.

Throughout the main living areas, luxury vinyl plank flooring adds a durable and stylish finish. The bathrooms feature tile, giving the home a polished look while also keeping each space practical.

Another thoughtful feature is the mudroom with built-ins, located off the two-car garage. This is a detail buyers often appreciate after moving in. It gives you a place for bags, shoes, sports gear, pet items, and daily essentials before entering the main living space.

The first floor does more than look good. It works well. That balance of style and function is what makes this home stand out.

Second Floor Primary Suite, Bedrooms, and Laundry

The second floor at 3223 W Paxton Ave continues the home’s strong focus on comfort and livability.

Two bedrooms share a Jack and Jill bathroom, which is a smart setup for family living. Each bedroom has access to the shared bath, while still maintaining separation and privacy. This layout works well for children, guests, or anyone who wants bedrooms that feel connected but not crowded.

A third upstairs bedroom includes its own en-suite bathroom. This is another feature that adds flexibility. It can work well for an older child, a long-term guest, or a private suite for visiting family.

The primary suite is one of the strongest parts of the home. It is spacious enough for a king-size bed and additional furniture, creating a true retreat at the end of the day.

The suite includes two large walk-in closets:

Walk-in Closet #1 10′7″ × 15′0″
Walk-in Closet #2 7′9″ × 9′8″

These closet sizes are impressive. They give buyers the storage they want without sacrificing the bedroom space itself.

The primary bathroom feels luxurious and well planned. It includes a standalone soaking tub, a large shower with both a rain shower and a regular showerhead, and dual vanities with extra storage.

This setup creates a spa-like feel while still being practical for daily routines. The dual vanities help keep the space organized. The extra storage is useful for towels, products, and everyday essentials. The separate tub and shower give the room a higher-end feel that buyers expect in a custom South Tampa home.

The large upstairs laundry room is another major convenience. Having laundry on the same floor as most of the bedrooms makes everyday chores easier. It reduces trips up and down the stairs and keeps the home more functional for busy households.

Together, the second-floor layout gives the home a strong mix of private retreats, flexible bedrooms, and smart storage.

Outdoor Living With a Large Backyard and Covered Lanai

Outdoor space is another reason to see 3223 W Paxton Ave in person.

The home sits on a deep lot with a large backyard and room for a pool. For many South Tampa buyers, room for a pool is a major priority. It gives homeowners the option to create a private outdoor retreat that fits their lifestyle.

The extended covered lanai runs across the back of the home. This creates a natural extension of the indoor living space and gives you a shaded area for outdoor dining, relaxing, and entertaining.

The lanai also includes plumbing for an outdoor kitchen. That opens the door for future upgrades and makes the backyard even more appealing for buyers who enjoy hosting.

Imagine watching a game outside, grilling with friends, or enjoying a quiet dinner on the covered lanai. The layout gives you the flexibility to create the outdoor setup you want.

The backyard can work for families, pets, entertaining, gardening, or future pool plans. It is not just extra space. It is usable space.

In South Tampa, where buyers often compare lot size, outdoor usability, and entertaining potential, this backyard is a major advantage.
